Where You Begin

Your Home Church starts as big as you earned

There's only one thing to grow: your congregation size. But not everyone starts at the same level. Your performance on the icebreakers on day 1 sets the size of your starting Home Church: place higher, and you begin with a bigger congregation. From there, all 14 groups race to grow the largest church by the final whistle.

The Upgrade Track

Your building grows with your population

As your congregation swells, you can upgrade to a bigger building. Each church size is a separate tier that raises your maximum capacity and gives a permanent earning bonus on every station reward. Members earned over capacity leave your church so keep upgrading.

Need a Hand

Blueprint hints & the safety net

📐 Buy a blueprint hint

Stuck on a Church Upgrade puzzle? Spend a slice of your population on a blueprint hint. Costs are 15% → 20% → 25% and must be bought in order but every group that finishes before you makes the hint 1% cheaper.

📐 Blueprint 2 of 3 · –20% population

🛟 The 10-member safety net

No matter what happens — a costly hint, a rival's theft, a failed event — your congregation size can never drop below 10 members. Every church survives to keep playing.

👥 Floor: 10 members · always
