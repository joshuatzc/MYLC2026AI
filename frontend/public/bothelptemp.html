Bot Commands & Help

Quick reference guide for the MYLC 2026 Bot

✨
Pro-tip: Use the persistent menu buttons at the bottom of the chat to trigger main operations instantly without typing commands.

Common Commands

/start Player

Initialise the bot, register your account, and choose or change your team group.

/help Player

Displays help instructions and links to this interactive guide.

Leader Commands

/becomeleader Leader

Elevate your role to Group Leader. Requires your group's secret admin password.

How to Play

  • 1

    Choose a Group

    Use the 🔄 Change Group button or run /start to choose which church group you represent.

  • 2

    Track Progress

    Tap ⛪ My Church to see your current church population and unlocked items. Tap 🗺️ My Journey to see your progression map.

  • 3

    Admin Actions

    If you're a Group Leader, tap 🔑 Admin Section to register upgrades, buy hints, or steal from other churches.
