Game Guide & Rules

Welcome to Build the Biggest Church — MYLC 2026

Objective

Your goal is to cooperate with your team to grow your church population to be the largest by the end of the game. Grow your church by unlocking levels at different stations.

Core Gameplay Loop

📍

Visit Stations

Travel with your team to various gameplay stations in the venue. Each station has a specific theme and upgrade requirements.

🧩

Solve Challenges & Hints

Complete challenges at stations to unlock hints and verify your prerequisites. You can purchase hints using a percentage of your church population.

⬆️

Upgrade Church Levels

Once you meet all requirements, the Group Leader can register a Level Upgrade at the station, permanently boosting your overall population.

⚡

Theft & Sabotage

Watch out! Upgrades can unlock theft options that let you steal populations from rival churches, turning the tide of the competition.

Key Rules

🔑 Station Prerequisites

You cannot upgrade a level unless you have completed the prerequisite levels from other stations. Check your status using 📋 Check Prerequisites in the bot!

👑 Leader Authorization

Only the authorized Group Leader can trigger actions like church level upgrades, purchasing hints, or executing theft. Coordinate with your leader before making key moves.
